# espeakng-loader license provenance

The 0.2.4 wheel omitted a license file. Upstream subsequently published the
MIT license at commit `0ddc87adf77e5850d7eeb542ac8a87d421b64daa` on November 1,
2025. Its `src/espeakng_loader/__init__.py` is identical to the installed 0.2.4
runtime after normalizing Windows CRLF to LF.

`146599e29be31bf17d99f0bcb7dbb2f92aef3d95` and the later licensed publication.
